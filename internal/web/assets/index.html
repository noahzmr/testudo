WiFi · wireless link quality

One card per radio with a computed link-quality grade (RSSI + SNR + TX-failure blend), live signal, PHY bitrate, and the full nl80211/iw counter set. The grade looks beyond raw signal: a strong-signal link with a high retry/failure rate still grades down.
